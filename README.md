# Lyès Kaci

Lawyer admitted to the Paris Bar, working primarily on international taxation, tax treaties and tax litigation.

I also conduct research on artificial intelligence and law, with a particular interest in the representation of legal rules, legal reasoning, verification of AI-generated legal conclusions and the interaction between legal systems and AI agents.

Author and editor of [Fiscalité internationale](https://fiscaliteinternationale.fr/), an independent publication devoted to international taxation, case law and related research.

## Public projects

### France Tax Treaty Directory

A public, versioned directory of tax treaties and related instruments concluded by France. The v1.0.0 snapshot covers **128 jurisdictions**, **255 instrument references** and **7 instruments signed but not yet in force**, with links to official sources.

[Web directory](https://fiscaliteinternationale.fr/conventions-fiscales/) · [Repository](https://github.com/Lyes-Kaci/france-tax-treaty-directory) · [Release v1.0.0](https://github.com/Lyes-Kaci/france-tax-treaty-directory/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

### Tax Structure Diagram Editor

A browser-based editor for representing legal and tax structures, ownership relationships and cross-border flows. Processing is local, the tool makes no automated legal conclusion, and diagrams can be exported as JSON, PNG, SVG or PDF.

[Live editor](https://fiscaliteinternationale.fr/schemas-fiscaux/) · [Repository](https://github.com/Lyes-Kaci/tax-structure-diagram-editor) · [Release v1.2.6](https://github.com/Lyes-Kaci/tax-structure-diagram-editor/releases/tag/v1.2.6)
